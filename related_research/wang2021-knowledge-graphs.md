# Knowledge Graphs for Scientific Literature Discovery

**Keywords:** knowledge graphs, literature discovery, semantic search, research navigation, graph neural networks
**Year:** 2021
**Authors:** Wang, K., Shen, Z., Huang, C., Wu, C. H., Dong, Y., & Kanakia, A.

## Summary

This research explores the use of knowledge graphs to represent relationships between scientific papers, including citation relationships, shared concepts, and research topics. The work demonstrates how graph-based approaches can improve literature discovery and recommendation.

## Key Contributions

- Novel knowledge graph construction methods for scientific literature
- Graph-based recommendation algorithms for related paper discovery
- Evaluation on large-scale citation networks

## Relevance

Knowledge graph approaches provide a powerful way to model and navigate citation relationships. This is highly relevant for systems that need to suggest related research based on complex semantic and citation relationships.
